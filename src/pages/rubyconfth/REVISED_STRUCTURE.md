## EMOTIONAL ARC

```
FEAR ──→ MENTOR ──→ MY DOUBT ──→ EVIDENCE ──→ HONEST GAPS ──→ INSIGHT ──→ CHOICE ──→ MY MISSION
  ↓         ↓           ↓            ↓              ↓             ↓          ↓           ↓
relatable  wisdom    vulnerable   surprising     honest        reframe    empower    inspire
```

The audience journey:
1. "I feel this fear too" (connection)
2. "There's wisdom here" (hope)
3. "She had my exact doubts" (trust)
4. "Wait, this is actually happening?" (surprise)
5. "She's being honest about problems" (credibility)
6. "I never thought of it this way" (insight)
7. "I have a choice" (agency)
8. "I want to be part of this" (belonging)
